Notula Meeting

Record in-person meetings on iPhone or Apple Watch. Notula transcribes and summarises them, and puts the dates that come up into your calendar. Processing stays in the EU by default.

Support

Questions, bugs or feature requests: open an issue at github.com/bahadirseyfi/notula/issues.

  • Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID settings: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Restore purchases: open the plans screen in Notula and tap "Restore purchases".
  • Delete your data: Notula → Settings → Data → "Delete my server data" and "Delete all local data".
